Who we are

Mid-sized on purpose

We sit in a deliberate middle ground. Larger than a one-person consultancy, so you are not dependent on a single person’s calendar. Smaller than the national integrators, so your account is not handed to whoever is available.

Our clients are typically established businesses with real operational systems: trades and construction, professional services, wholesale and logistics, healthcare and community organisations, and resource-sector suppliers.

What we stand for

Four things we do not compromise on

Honesty over the sale

If a cheaper or simpler option is right for you, that is what we will recommend — even when it means a smaller job for us.

Evidence over opinion

Decisions about your systems should rest on data and testing, not on whoever argues loudest in the meeting.

Security by default

Access control, backups and patching are designed in from the start, not bolted on after an incident.

Handover over dependency

You get documentation, training and access. We would rather earn the next job than lock you into this one.

How we work together

What an engagement actually looks like

First conversation

We ask about the business, the systems and the constraint. Often we can tell you in that call whether we are the right people.

Discovery & proposal

If it looks like a fit, we scope the work — systems reviewed, requirements captured, options costed — and send a written proposal.

Delivery

Work is broken into stages. You see progress at each stage and can change priorities between them without starting again.

Handover & aftercare

Documentation, training for your team, and a support arrangement sized to what you need.

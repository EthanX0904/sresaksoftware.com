How we think

Before we build anything, we find out what is holding the business up.

Most problems we are called about are not exotic. Backups that have never been restored. Three people sharing an admin login. A process only one person knows. That is where the real risk sits — and it is usually cheap to fix.

  • A written scope and fixed price before any work starts
  • Access, backups and monitoring reviewed first
  • Everything documented and handed over — you own it
  • We say no to work that does not make sense for you

Fit

Straight about who we are for

Working with the wrong supplier wastes a year. Here is where we do well, and where we do not.

A good fit if

  • 5–200 staff with real operational systems
  • You want someone to tell you the truth about what you have
  • There is a process that hurts and a budget to fix it
  • You value documentation and being able to hold the keys
  • Perth based, or happy working remotely with a Perth team

Probably not a fit if

  • You need someone on site every day (we are not a helpdesk body shop)
  • The lowest hourly rate is the deciding factor
  • You want a build with no involvement from your team
  • A 3-year lock-in contract is the goal
  • You need 24/7 phone coverage in five time zones

Questions

The ones people ask first

How do you charge?
Fixed-scope projects are quoted in writing after a discovery conversation — sometimes free, sometimes a small paid assessment if the environment needs real investigation. Ongoing support is a monthly retainer sized on users and devices.
Do you work outside Perth?
Yes. We are based in Perth and most clients are in WA, but we support remote teams across Australia and work remotely by default.
We already have an IT provider. Is that a problem?
Usually not. A lot of our work is project-based alongside an existing provider — software, a cloud migration, a security uplift, reporting — without touching your support arrangement.
What size business do you work with?
Generally 5 to 200 staff. Big enough to have real systems and obligations, small enough that we can talk to the people who decide.
How soon can you start?
Discovery within one to two weeks. Delivery dates are confirmed in the proposal, not guessed at on the phone.
Do you subcontract?
For specialist work — licensed electrical, structural, legal — yes, and we coordinate it so you still have one point of contact.

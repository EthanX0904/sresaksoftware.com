Questions

What people ask us first

How do you charge?
Most projects are fixed-scope, fixed-price, quoted after a paid or free discovery conversation depending on size. Ongoing support is a monthly retainer based on the number of users and devices. Hourly work is available when a fixed scope genuinely doesn’t make sense.
Do you work with businesses outside Perth?
Yes. We are based in Perth and most of our clients are in Western Australia, but we support remote teams across Australia.
We already have an IT provider. Can you still help?
Often, yes. Plenty of our work is project-based alongside an existing provider — software, cloud migration, security uplift or reporting — without touching the support arrangement.
How quickly can you start?
Discovery usually within one to two weeks. Delivery dates depend on current capacity and are confirmed in the proposal.
What size business do you work with?
Typically 5 to 200 staff. Big enough to have real systems and compliance obligations, small enough that we can talk to the decision makers directly.
Do you offer a warranty on delivered work?
Yes — defects in delivered software or configuration are fixed at no charge within the agreed warranty period at handover.

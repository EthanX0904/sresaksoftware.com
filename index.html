How we think

Before we build anything, we find out what is holding the business up.

Most problems we are called about are not exotic. Backups that have never been restored. Three people sharing an admin login. A process that only one person knows. That is usually where the real risk sits — and it is cheap to fix.

  • A written scope and fixed price before any work starts
  • Access, backups and monitoring reviewed first
  • Everything documented and handed over — you own it
  • We say no to work that does not make sense for you

Questions

The ones people ask first

How do you charge?
Fixed-scope projects are quoted in writing after a discovery conversation — sometimes free, sometimes a small paid assessment if the environment needs real investigation. Ongoing support is a monthly retainer sized on users and devices. Hourly work only where a fixed scope genuinely makes no sense.
Do you work outside Perth?
Yes. We are based in Perth and most clients are in WA, but we support remote teams across Australia and work remotely by default.
We already have an IT provider. Is that a problem?
Usually not. A lot of our work is project-based alongside an existing provider — software, a cloud migration, a security uplift, reporting — without touching your support arrangement.
What size business do you work with?
Generally 5 to 200 staff. Big enough to have real systems and obligations, small enough that we can talk to the people who decide.
How soon can you start?
Discovery within one to two weeks. Delivery dates are confirmed in the proposal, not guessed at on the phone.

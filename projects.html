Professional servicesCase summary

Practice management automation

The situation
A mid-sized professional services firm ran scheduling, invoicing and reporting across three disconnected systems.
What we delivered
A single internal platform replacing three tools — job scheduling, timesheets and automated invoicing, integrated with their accounting package.

Hours of manual administration removed each week, and month-end reporting that runs itself.

Wholesale & logisticsCase summary

Inventory and order visibility

The situation
Stock accuracy depended on a nightly manual reconciliation and staff could not see availability while on the phone with customers.
What we delivered
A live inventory and order platform with barcode-driven stock movements and a customer-facing availability view, integrated with their ERP.
Result
Stock discrepancies found same-day instead of weeks later, and fewer lost sales.

Resources & industrialCase summary

Field data capture

The situation
Site inspections were captured on paper and re-typed at the office, with photos lost between the two.
What we delivered
A tablet-based field capture app that works offline, attaches photos and location to each record and syncs automatically when back in range.
Result
Reports issued the same day, with a defensible audit trail behind every record.

Questions

Practical ones

What does a project cost?
It depends on scope, and a range that means nothing wastes your time. After discovery you get a fixed-price proposal broken into stages, with assumptions and exclusions spelled out.
Who actually does the work?
The people you meet. We bring in qualified specialists when the work needs them — licensed electrical, structural, legal — and we coordinate them so you still have one point of contact.
Can you take over from another provider?
Yes. We document the environment first, then plan the changeover so support never has a gap.
What happens to our data if we leave?
It is yours. Exports, documentation and access are handed over as part of any engagement — we do not hold your business hostage.

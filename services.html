Engagement models

Three ways to work with us

Fixed-scope project

A defined outcome, a written proposal and a fixed price. Best for software builds, migrations and remediation work.

Monthly retainer

An agreed number of support hours, a named contact and priority response. Best for ongoing IT and system care.

Advisory / discovery

A short paid engagement to investigate, document and recommend — useful before committing to a large project.

FAQ

Practical questions

What does a typical project cost?
It depends on scope, and we would rather give you a real number than a range that means nothing. After discovery you receive a fixed-price proposal broken down by stage, with assumptions and exclusions listed.
Do you subcontract?
For specialist work such as licensed electrical, structural or legal advice we bring in the appropriate qualified party and coordinate them — you still have one point of contact.
Can you take over from another provider?
Yes. We document the existing environment first, then plan the transition so there is no gap in support.
What about our data?
Your data stays yours. Access is role-based, backups are tested, and at the end of an engagement you receive exports and documentation.
